Terms of Work

agias.dev · effective 2026-09-06

These terms apply when you ask AGIAS to build something. They are short because the work itself is recorded: every decision goes into a ledger you receive. Where these terms and a written agreement for your project differ, the written agreement wins.

1. What you can ask for

  • GENERATIVE SYSTEMS: a machine that keeps generating for you (images, motion, text, data) on a schedule you set.
  • NIGHTLY RESEARCH: a machine that scans your field at night and keeps a reasoned ledger of what to adopt and what to skip.
  • AUTONOMOUS OPERATIONS: a secretary AI, nightly jobs, and a window that receives requests, running within a budget you set.

2. How a request runs

  1. You send what you want to make, in plain words.
  2. We set the direction. The secretary AI drafts a specification. You confirm it. Work does not start before that.
  3. You receive a running prototype, not a slide.
  4. You adjust in chat. Every decision is recorded in the ledger.
  5. Delivery: the running machine and its ledger. A nightly check after delivery can be built on request; its fee is agreed when the project is agreed.

Changes after the specification is confirmed may change the fee and the schedule. We tell you before we continue.

3. How the work is done

AI systems build under human direction. Humans decide what to build and when to stop. You are told which models contributed. Only the AI secretary communicates with you. Nothing is published without your approval.

We do not promise delivery dates or response times unless they are written in the agreement for your project.

4. Fees and payment

  • The fee and the payment schedule are agreed in writing (email is fine) before work starts.
  • Money moves only with human approval on our side, and we never charge you without your written agreement.
  • Invoices are due within 30 days unless agreed otherwise. Bank charges are borne by the payer.
  • Subscriptions to AI platforms and other services the machine needs (accounts, API plans, registrations) are taken out and paid by you, in your name. We tell you what is needed before you subscribe.

5. Ownership

  • Once the agreed fee is paid, you own the delivered machine and its ledger.
  • We keep the right to reuse general techniques, tools, and code that are not specific to you.
  • Open-source and third-party components stay under their own licenses. Output produced with third-party AI services is subject to those services' terms.
  • You confirm that the material you give us may lawfully be used for your project.

6. Confidentiality

What you tell us about your business stays between us, except as needed to do the work (the AI services and providers named in the Privacy Policy) or as required by law. We may name you as a client only with your permission.

7. Warranty and liability

  • We deliver working software. Defects you report within 14 days of delivery are corrected at no charge. Anything else is agreed per project.
  • Beyond that, the machine is delivered as it is. AI-based systems can produce wrong or unexpected output; you decide where it is used. Do not use it where failure could endanger life, health, or safety.
  • Our total liability for a project is limited to the fee paid for that project, and applies only to defects in what we delivered. We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, for lost profit, or for the output of AI systems in the use you chose for them.

8. Ending a project

Either side can end a project with written notice. You pay for the work done up to that point, and you receive what was built and its ledger so far.

9. Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Japan. Disputes go to the Tokyo District Court as the court of first instance. The Japanese text of these terms prevails if the two versions differ.

10. Contact

ai@agias.dev

The operator's business name, address, and phone number are provided without delay on request.
