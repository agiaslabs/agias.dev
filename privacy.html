Privacy Policy

agias.dev · effective 2026-09-06 (draft, pending review)

This policy explains what AGIAS collects when you visit agias.dev or ask us for work, why we collect it, which services process it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to be read, not skimmed.

1. Who we are

AGIAS is operated by a sole proprietor in Tokyo, Japan. We do not publish the operator's personal name on this site by design. The operator's business name, address, and phone number are provided to you without delay on request.

Contact for everything in this policy: ai@agias.dev (read by the operator and by the operator's AI secretary).

2. What we collect

2.1 When you visit the site

  • No cookies. No analytics. No advertising. No account.
  • Your language choice (English or Japanese) is kept in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device.
  • The hosting provider (Vercel) may keep standard server logs, including IP addresses, for security and operation of the service. We do not use them to identify you.
  • The typeface is loaded from Google Fonts. When your browser fetches it, Google receives your IP address under Google's privacy policy.

2.2 When an AI agent or a person uses the reception endpoint (/api/a2a)

  • The endpoint stores nothing. The sender's IP address is held in the server's memory for at most 10 minutes to limit the number of requests, then discarded. It is not written to disk or to any log of ours.
  • A message sent with the "leave-message" skill is forwarded to the operator as incoming mail, with its text and time. If forwarding is not configured, the message is rejected and nothing is kept.

2.3 When you ask us for work

  • What you send us: your name or organization, email address, the brief, and any material (documents, data, images, sound, logos) you choose to share.
  • What we create with you: the specification, the prototype, the finished machine, and its ledger (the record of what was decided and why).
  • Correspondence about the work and, if applicable, invoicing details.

3. Why we collect it

  • To answer you, write the specification, build, deliver, and support what we built.
  • To keep the ledger, which is part of what you receive.
  • To invoice and to meet legal obligations (tax and accounting records).
  • To keep the site and the reception endpoint safe from abuse.

We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell it.

4. AI services and transfers outside Japan

Your brief, your material, and our correspondence may be processed by AI services under the operator's direction:

  • Anthropic (Claude), Google (Gemini), and OpenAI (GPT). Their servers are located outside Japan, mainly in the United States. Each processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Local models running on the operator's own machine. These do not send data anywhere.
By asking us for work, you consent to this processing, including the transfer of your information to these providers outside Japan. Where a provider offers a setting not to use customer data for training, we use it. We cannot control a provider beyond its published terms. If you do not want a particular provider used for your request, tell us and we will use another or work locally.

Only the AI secretary communicates with you. Other AI systems never contact you directly. Nothing is published without your approval.

5. Who else sees it

  • Nobody, except the AI providers above (processing on our behalf), the hosting provider (server logs), and a payment or invoicing service if one is used for your project.
  • Authorities, when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

  • Messages forwarded from the reception endpoint: kept as incoming mail for up to 90 days, then deleted, unless they became a request for work. The endpoint itself keeps nothing.
  • Material you sent for a project: for the duration of the project and up to one year after delivery, then deleted. Ask, and it is deleted earlier.
  • The ledger: handed over with the machine. We keep a copy as our own record of the work, with your personal information reduced to what is needed to identify the project.
  • Invoices and accounting records: as long as Japanese law requires.

7. Your rights

You can ask us to show you what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Write to ai@agias.dev. We may ask you to confirm your identity. We answer as soon as we reasonably can; we do not promise a fixed response time. If Japanese law gives you further rights, you have them too.

8. How we protect it

  • Your information is kept in the operator's own records (an OBSIDIAN vault) on devices with full-disk encryption turned on (FileVault on the Mac; iOS data protection on the phone), not in a shared or public system. Copies synced through iCloud are encrypted by Apple in transit and at rest.
  • Access is limited to the operator and the AI secretary acting under the operator's direction.
  • Money and publishing require human approval. Nothing leaves without a record.

9. Children

The site and the services are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

10. Changes

When this policy changes, the new version is posted here with a new effective date. Material changes that affect an ongoing project are sent to you by email.
